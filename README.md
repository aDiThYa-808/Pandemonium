
**Pandemonium: Survival Horror** is a chilling first-person shooter game where you wake up trapped inside a mysterious maze with nothing but a gun and a cryptic letter. Your mission: survive the nightmarish maze filled with strange, hostile clowns and find the elusive door called **THE TRUTH** to escape.

---

## 🎮 Core Gameplay

- Explore a dark, eerie maze from a first-person perspective  
- Shoot and survive against bizarre clown enemies lurking in the shadows  
- Search for clues and navigate the maze to locate **THE TRUTH** door and escape

- Developed with **Unity Engine**

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Install from the Google Play Store  
2. Launch the game and explore the maze  
3. Use your gun to defend against enemies  
4. Locate the door named **THE TRUTH** to escape
